# Industry: Dental Clinic

## Typical pains
- After-hours WhatsApp inquiries from prospective patients sit unanswered until morning.
- Reception staff spend significant time on appointment reminders and rescheduling.
- High-value services (Invisalign, veneers) require longer consultative chats that the front desk can't fully handle.
- Reviews are infrequent given satisfied patient volume; negative reviews go unanswered.

## Typical opportunities
- `whatsapp-receptionist` — most common fit. Inquiries spike after hours.
- `lead-qualification-agent` — for clinics running paid ads.
- `review-automation` — broadly relevant.
- `ai-content-engine` — for clinics with Instagram presence but inconsistent posting.

## Baselines (for ROI Estimator)
- Average appointment net value (UAE): $90–$150 routine, $400–$1500 cosmetic.
- Typical inquiry conversion: 25–40% in business hours; drops to <10% after hours without automation.
- After-hours inquiry share: 30–45%.
- Expected uplift from `whatsapp-receptionist`: 8–20 additional booked appointments/month for a clinic of this profile.

## Tone (for Email Writer + WhatsApp Writer)
Direct, peer-to-peer, warm but not chatty. Reference specific services the clinic names on their site (Invisalign, veneers, pediatric, etc.). Use clinic vocabulary correctly (patient, consultation, appointment — not "user", "session", "booking"). Avoid medical-claim-style language ("transform your smile", "revolutionary").

## Banned for this industry
- Claims about clinical outcomes.
- Anything that could read as medical advice.
- "10x your appointments" — sounds spammy in healthcare context.
