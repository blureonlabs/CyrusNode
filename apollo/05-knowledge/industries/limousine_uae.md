# Industry: Limousine / Chauffeur Service (UAE)

> One of the strongest verticals in the UAE for AI services: high transaction value, WhatsApp-heavy booking, almost no AI agencies targeting it yet.

## Typical pains
- After-hours WhatsApp / phone inquiries for airport pickups and weddings go unanswered; each missed booking is $300–$1500 lost.
- Last-minute changes (delayed flights, cancellations, address changes) eat dispatcher hours.
- Corporate accounts demand invoicing + monthly reporting; most operators manually rebuild this from WhatsApp threads each month.
- No-show clients waste fleet capacity that could be reassigned.
- Drivers operate from group chats, not a dispatch system — instructions get lost.

## Typical opportunities
- `whatsapp-receptionist` — strongest fit. Routes inquiries by service (airport / wedding / corporate / hourly), captures pickup details, books into a calendar.
- `lead-qualification-agent` — for operators running Meta/Google Ads on "airport transfer Dubai".
- `auto-followup-engine` — for missed bookings and corporate prospect re-engagement.
- `internal-rag-knowledge` — for larger operators (≥ 30 staff) where SOPs (insurance, RTA permits, dress code) live in disconnected docs.

## Baselines (for ROI Estimator)
- Average trip value (UAE):
  - Airport transfer one-way: $80–$200
  - Hourly chauffeur: $50–$120/hr
  - Wedding / event: $500–$2000 per booking
  - Corporate monthly retainer: $2000–$8000
- Typical inquiry → booking conversion: 30–50% in business hours; drops to 5–15% after hours without automation.
- After-hours inquiry share: 40–60% (Dubai airport runs cluster 6pm–2am).
- Expected uplift from `whatsapp-receptionist`: 15–35 additional confirmed bookings/month for an operator of this profile.

## Tone (for Email Writer + WhatsApp Writer)
Crisp and operator-to-operator. Reference specific service tiers the operator names on their site (Mercedes S-Class, Cadillac Escalade, Maybach, corporate, airport meet-and-greet). Mention RTA-licensed only when relevant. Avoid generic "luxury experience" language — they sell that themselves; speak operations.

## Banned for this industry
- Promises about safety or licensing outcomes ("we'll make your operations safer") — regulated by RTA.
- Anything implying compliance or legal advice.

## Outreach anchor candidates
When Apollo's BizIntel produces a summary for a UAE limo company, the email writer should anchor on at least two of:
- Specific named fleet model from their site.
- Named service tier (airport transfer, hourly, weddings, corporate).
- Visible WhatsApp number without chatbot evidence.
- Recent Instagram post if posting cadence is regular.
- Absence of online booking widget when site has a "Contact" CTA.
