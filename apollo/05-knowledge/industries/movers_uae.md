# Industry: Packers & Movers (UAE)

> One of the highest-ACV verticals for Apollo in the UAE. Big-ticket transactions, high-intent buyers, WhatsApp-native.

## Typical pains
- Late-evening / weekend WhatsApp quote requests get missed; each lost quote is AED 1,500–8,000 in revenue.
- Quote-to-survey conversion is poor because the back-and-forth happens in WhatsApp threads with no follow-up trigger.
- Survey visit scheduling is manual and burns admin hours.
- Corporate relocation accounts demand reporting (jobs/month, claims, on-time rate) that nobody compiles.
- Lead leakage from Google to competitors with faster first response.

## Typical opportunities
- `whatsapp-receptionist` — strongest fit. Captures inquiry, qualifies (origin / destination / size / date / villa vs apartment), books a survey, sends quote follow-up sequence.
- `lead-qualification-agent` — for movers running paid Google or Meta ads.
- `auto-followup-engine` — for cold quotes ("hey, did you decide?" 48h later).
- `internal-rag-knowledge` — for ops at scale (>20 staff) to answer crew questions about insurance, RTA, customs.

## Baselines (for ROI Estimator)
- Avg local move (Dubai apartment): AED 1,500–4,000
- Avg villa move within Dubai: AED 4,000–12,000
- Avg interemirate (Dubai ↔ Abu Dhabi): AED 3,000–9,000
- Avg international relocation: AED 8,000–40,000
- Typical inquiry → quote rate: 60–80% in business hours; ≤30% after hours
- Typical quote → survey rate: 25–45%
- After-hours inquiry share: 35–55%
- Expected uplift from `whatsapp-receptionist`: 12–25 extra confirmed surveys/month

## Tone (for Email Writer + WhatsApp Writer)
Crisp, operator-to-operator. Mention specific service tiers from their site (local, villa, office, interemirate, international, packing only, storage). Reference customs/insurance only if their site does. Avoid "professional" "premium" "best-in-class" — too generic. They sell on price + speed + zero damage. Speak ops.

## Banned for this industry
- Vague promises ("more bookings"). Movers care about quote→survey→close ratios.
- Anything implying insurance or compliance guidance.
