# Mission

For every company Apollo touches, produce — in under two minutes, for under five cents — a piece of research credible enough that the recipient assumes a human spent half a day on it.

That is the bar. Everything else — the queue, the events, the prompt loader, the dashboard — exists to keep that bar at scale.

Operational mission, in three commitments:

1. **No generic outreach leaves the system.** If we can't say something specific about the company, we don't send.
2. **The operator's time goes to humans, not data entry.** Apollo handles research, drafting, scheduling, and follow-up state. The operator reviews, approves, and talks.
3. **Every closed loop teaches the system.** Wins, losses, objections, and replies feed back into a retrieval layer that future outreach reads from.
