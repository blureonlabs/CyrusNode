# Business Model

## V1 — Internal tool (now)

Apollo is the operator's competitive advantage as a freelance AI consultant. It is not sold. Its value is captured in:

- Higher close rate per outreach (researched > generic).
- Higher deal sizes (concrete proposals > vague chatbot pitches).
- Lower opportunity cost (operator's hours go to humans, not data entry).

Pricing model the operator is selling *to clients* (not Apollo itself):

- **Free pilot (loss leader)**: build one high-value automation for free for 2–3 carefully chosen Dubai businesses to seed case studies before the trip.
- **Discovery audit**: $500–$1500 fixed. Apollo's audit + a 60-minute consultation. Often credited toward the full engagement.
- **Build engagements**: $5k–$25k per project (website + AI agent, WhatsApp receptionist, lead-qual bot, internal RAG).
- **Retainer**: $1500–$5000/month for ongoing maintenance, additions, and oversight.

## V2 — Closed SaaS for AI consultants

Once the operator has 5+ live clients and a documented playbook, Apollo opens to a closed beta of other one-person AI consultants. Pricing model:

- **$199/mo** Solo plan: 100 researched companies + sequencer.
- **$499/mo** Pro plan: 500 researched companies + multi-channel + meeting coach.
- **$1499/mo** Agency plan: unlimited + multi-seat + branded proposals.

LLM costs are the COGS. Margin works only because of the determinism-before-LLM and aggressive caching enforced by [[../CLAUDE]] §6.

## V3 — Open SaaS / API

Once V2 has 50 paying customers and ARR > $25k/mo, open public signup. Possible API tier for AI-forward agencies to embed Apollo's research in their own tools.

## What we will not do

- Take VC funding pre-V2 close.
- Pay for cold-traffic ads. Distribution is content and the operator's own outreach (eat our own dogfood).
- Build for enterprise. The product shape is tuned for small operators; enterprise sales has different requirements we won't entertain until V3+.
