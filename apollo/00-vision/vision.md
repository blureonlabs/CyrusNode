# Vision

A one-person AI consultancy should be able to compete with — and beat — a 20-person AI agency on the quality and specificity of its outreach.

Apollo is the platform that makes that true. It collapses the work that fills four roles in a typical agency (researcher, SDR, copywriter, sales engineer) into one operator with one inbox and one dashboard. The work the agency does in a week, Apollo does in an afternoon. The work the agency *says* it does but actually skips — real research before the first message — Apollo does on every lead.

The end state: a researched, audited, opportunity-mapped dossier exists for every meaningful business in our target market before the operator ever reaches out. Outreach stops being prospecting and starts being delivery of work already done.
