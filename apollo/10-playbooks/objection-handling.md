# Objection Handling

> A living document. Every meeting feeds new objections into Apollo via `deal.outcome_recorded` → Qdrant `objections` collection. The Meeting Coach retrieves from here next time.

## Top objections (initial seed list)

### "We tried a chatbot before and patients hated it."
- **Why it lands**: most off-the-shelf bots are tree-based and dead-end conversations.
- **Response**: "Most off-the-shelf bots are linear scripts — they hate them for a reason. This one is conversational, trained on your actual services page, and hands off to a human within 30 seconds if it gets out of depth. Want to see a 60-second handoff demo on your own site copy?"
- **Worked?**: pending data.

### "Sounds expensive."
- **Response**: "Fair — the question is whether the math works. Your dossier suggests N missed inquiries per month. Even at the low end of the range, payback is around 6 weeks. If we agree on a 30-day pilot with a defined success metric, we de-risk most of that."
- **Worked?**: pending data.

### "We don't have time to set this up."
- **Response**: "That's the point of how I work — the setup is on my side. From your side it's a 30-minute kickoff and one Slack channel. Two weeks later it's live."
- **Worked?**: pending data.

### "Are you in Dubai?"
- **Response**: "I'm based remotely with regular Dubai trips — next one is <date>. We can do the kickoff and pilot review in person and the build remote. Most of my UAE clients prefer this rhythm."
- **Worked?**: pending data.

### "Can we do this in Arabic too?"
- **Response (today)**: "English-only for V1. Arabic support is on the roadmap; I'd be honest about the timing rather than overpromise."
- **Worked?**: pending data.

### "Send me a proposal and I'll get back to you."
- **Why it lands**: classic stall.
- **Response**: "Will do. Quick check: what would you want to see in the proposal to be able to say yes — outcomes, timeline, references? I'd rather get this right than send the wrong shape."
- **Worked?**: pending data.

### "Our IT will block this."
- **Response**: "Makes sense. The way it integrates is <X> — happy to talk to your IT person directly so we don't waste your time relaying. Can you intro me?"
- **Worked?**: pending data.

### "We're already using <competitor>."
- **Response**: "Got it. What's working with <competitor> and what's the thing you wish were different? If there's no gap, I'd rather tell you that than push."
- **Worked?**: pending data.

## How to use this file
- Read once. Internalize the structure (acknowledge → reframe → ask).
- After every call, add new objections + responses + whether they worked.
- The Meeting Coach pulls from this corpus at prep time. Stale objections decay; recent ones rank higher.
