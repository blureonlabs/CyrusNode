# Discovery Call Playbook

> 30 minutes. Three jobs: confirm the dossier, find the buyer's specific pain, agree on a success metric.

## Pre-call (5 min, from Apollo's brief)
- Read [[meeting-coach]] output once.
- Re-skim the prospect's homepage and last 3 Instagram posts.
- Open the proposal one-pager so it's ready to walk through.

## Call structure
### 0–3 min — warm open
- Reference the specific thing the prospect mentioned in their reply or in their site.
- "Before I show you anything — quick check: am I right that <X> is the biggest manual-work bucket right now?"

### 3–10 min — confirm + dig
- Confirm or correct the dossier's understanding of their business.
- Ask about volumes ("how many WhatsApp messages a week comes through this number?").
- Ask about money ("what's a new patient worth to the clinic?"). Don't proxy.

### 10–18 min — propose + math
- Walk through the one top opportunity (just one).
- Use Apollo's ROI math live: "If we capture even 10 of those after-hours messages and book half, that's $X/month."
- Show the proposal one-pager.

### 18–25 min — objections
- Pull the top 1–2 from Apollo's brief. Address proactively.
- Listen for ones Apollo didn't predict; log these later.

### 25–30 min — commit
- Use the closing question from Apollo's brief.
- Frame as a success metric pilot: "If we hit N qualified appointments in 30 days, would you green-light a 90-day rollout?"
- Schedule a follow-up: if proposal accepted in principle, next step is contract; otherwise, a check-in in two weeks with a tiny POC.

## After the call (5 min)
- Log outcome via `POST /meetings/{id}/outcome`.
- Log every objection raised, including ones Apollo didn't predict.
- This is what makes Apollo better next time.
