# Personas

## P1 — The Operator (V1 primary)

**Profile**: AI engineer, ~5 years experience. Runs Apollo solo as a freelance consultancy. Comfortable with Rust, TypeScript, Postgres, Qdrant, FastAPI, LLM tooling. Currently employed full-time (see legal note in [[../PROJECT_BIBLE]] §9).

**Goals**:
- Land 5–10 paid Dubai engagements before in-person trip.
- Establish 2–3 free-pilot lighthouse clients with strong case studies.
- Minimize time spent on outreach mechanics; maximize time on conversations.

**Frustrations**:
- Generic AI-agency tools that hallucinate research.
- Cost-per-call blowouts when an agent retries a flaky LLM response.
- Tools that lock him out of the prompts they use.

**What he expects from Apollo**:
- Open prompts in Markdown. Owns the moat.
- Real cost numbers per company. No mystery billing.
- One-click replay when a research run fails halfway.
- A queue he can clear in 30 minutes a day.

## P2 — The Prospect (recipient of outreach)

**Profile**: Founder, COO, or marketing lead at a Dubai SMB (5–200 employees). Industries vary: clinics, real estate, jewellery, F&B, travel. Has WhatsApp Business + Instagram. May or may not have an in-house tech person.

**State at first contact**:
- Has received 5+ generic AI pitches this month and ignored all of them.
- Has heard "AI" enough to be curious and skeptical.
- Will respond to a message that demonstrates the sender understands their actual business.

**What turns them off**:
- Templated openers ("I came across your business...").
- Promises of "10x revenue" with no specifics.
- Pitches that don't reference what they actually sell.

**What earns the meeting**:
- A short message that names one specific thing about their business (a product, a campaign, a gap).
- A short proposal that names a specific automation tied to a specific recurring task.
- Proof the sender has worked with similar businesses (case studies, even small).
